# Multi-Source Search Strategy

To deliver a high-quality research brief, use a diverse range of sources and cross-reference findings.

## Phase 1: General Discovery (Web Search)
- Start with `web_search` to identify industry trends, major players, and common terminology.
- Identify 3-4 key URLs for deeper investigation.

## Phase 2: Deep Dive (Web Fetch)
- Use `web_fetch.py` to extract detailed content from the URLs identified in Phase 1.
- Focus on technical documentation, whitepapers, and in-depth articles.

## Phase 3: Live Data (GitHub CLI)
- For technical topics, use `github_cli.py` to gather:
  - Repository activity (stargazers, forks, recent commits).
  - Community health (issue response times, PR frequency).
  - Version history and changelogs.

## Phase 4: Cross-Verification
- Compare statistics across different sources (e.g., GitHub stats vs. marketing claims on a website).
- Highlight discrepancies in your research brief.
