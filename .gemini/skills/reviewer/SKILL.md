# Reviewer Skill

## Purpose
Elevate draft content to publication-ready quality through a systematic editorial review and fact-check.

## Workflow
1. **Fact Check**: Compare the draft against the original research brief. Ensure every claim is supported and every statistic is accurate.
2. **Structural Audit**: Verify that the flow is logical and the headings are informative.
3. **Style Polish**: Refine the writing for clarity, conciseness, and active voice.
4. **Formatting Check**: Ensure sources are correctly bulleted at the end and markdown is used properly for code blocks and emphasis.

## Review Checklist
- [ ] **Accuracy**: Does it perfectly reflect the research findings?
- [ ] **Audience**: Is the tone appropriate for technical practitioners?
- [ ] **Structure**: Are there 5-6 clear sections with proper headers?
- [ ] **Polish**: Is the writing crisp and free of errors?
- [ ] **Sources**: Are all source URLs from the researcher preserved?

## Output Structure: Polished Blog
Return the finalized, publication-ready blog post. Do not add conversational filler; only provide the markdown content of the post itself.
