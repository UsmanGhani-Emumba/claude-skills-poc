# Sample Research Brief Output

**Input:** "Write a blog post about remote work trends in 2025"

**Output:**

```markdown
# Research Brief: Remote Work Trends 2025

## Key Facts
- 58% of US workers have remote work options (Source: McKinsey, 2024)
- Hybrid work is the dominant model, with 3 days in-office being most common
- "Coffee badging" phenomenon: employees showing up briefly to be seen, then leaving
- AI tools are reshaping remote collaboration (async video, smart scheduling)

## Recent Developments
- Major tech companies (Amazon, Google) pushing return-to-office in late 2024
- Counter-trend: 40% of workers say they'd quit rather than return full-time (Gallup)
- "Hushed hybrid" - managers quietly allowing remote work despite company policy
- Rise of "workcations" - blending remote work with travel

## Statistics & Data
- Remote workers report 25% higher job satisfaction (Buffer State of Remote Work)
- Companies save avg $11,000/year per remote employee (Global Workplace Analytics)
- 67% of workers want flexibility over pay raises (FlexJobs Survey, 2024)
- Productivity metrics show no significant difference office vs remote (Stanford study)

## Interesting Angles
- The power shift: workers demanding flexibility vs companies demanding presence
- AI as the great enabler of async work
- Mental health tradeoffs: isolation vs commute stress

## Sources
1. [McKinsey Future of Work Report](https://mckinsey.com) - Comprehensive workforce analysis
2. [Buffer State of Remote Work 2024](https://buffer.com) - Annual worker survey
3. [Gallup Workplace Trends](https://gallup.com) - Employee sentiment data
4. [Stanford WFH Research](https://stanford.edu) - Productivity studies
```

## What Makes This Good

- **5+ credible sources** from authoritative organizations
- **Multiple statistics** with clear attribution
- **Recent data** (2024 sources for 2025 trends)
- **Interesting angles** identified for the Writer to explore
- **Clean structure** that's easy for the Writer skill to consume
