# Comprehensive Blog Quality Checklist

## 1. Grammatical & Linguistic Precision
- **Prepositional Accuracy:** Check every preposition (in, on, at, by, with, etc.). AI often makes subtle errors in prepositional phrases that break the natural "idiomatic" flow of English.
- **Active vs. Passive Voice:** Ensure at least 80% of sentences use the active voice. Convert "The data was analyzed by the team" to "The team analyzed the data."
- **Tense Consistency:** Verify that the blog doesn't flip-flop between past and present tense within the same section.
- **Subject-Verb Agreement:** Ensure singular/plural subjects match their verbs, especially in complex sentences with intervening clauses.

## 2. Structural Integrity (Human-Centric)
- **Sentence Length Variety:**
  - **The "Punch" Sentence:** Use short, 3-7 word sentences for impact.
  - **The "Flow" Sentence:** Use medium, 12-20 word sentences for narrative.
  - **The "Complex" Sentence:** Max 30-35 words. Never have two 30-word sentences in a row.
- **Paragraph Density:** 
  - Max 4-5 sentences per paragraph.
  - If a paragraph looks like a "wall of text," break it up.
  - Single-sentence paragraphs are allowed for emphasis.
- **Coherence:** Every paragraph must start with a transition or a clear topic sentence that links to the previous idea.

## 3. Eliminating the "AI Footprint"
- **Hyphen & Dash Audit:** AI overuses em-dashes (—) and hyphens (-) to connect loosely related thoughts. 
  - **Rule:** If a sentence has more than one em-dash, rewrite it using a semicolon or two separate sentences.
  - Avoid using hyphens as a crutch for complex sentence structures.
- **Emoji Restriction:** 
  - **Absolute Ban:** 🚀, ✨, 💡, 🛠️, 📈, 🤖. These are "AI hallmarks."
  - **Rule:** Only use emojis inside Callout Boxes or if specifically requested. Never use them in headings or as bullet point markers.
- **Word Choice (The "Forbidden" List):** Strip out these AI-favorite words:
  - *Delve, Unlock, Comprehensive, Transformative, In today's fast-paced world, Navigating, Synergistic, Mastering, Look no further.*
- **Intro/Outro Patterns:** Remove the "AI Wrap-up" tone. Avoid "In conclusion," "To summarize," or "Ultimately." End with a callback to the hook or a provocative final thought.

## 4. Logical Flow & Readability
- **The "Read Aloud" Test:** Does the sentence sound like a human would actually say it? If it's too rhythmic or too perfect, add some "human" variation.
- **Vocabulary:** Use concrete nouns and strong verbs instead of abstract adjectives. (e.g., Instead of "a very large improvement," use "a massive leap").
- **Clarity:** Every technical term must be explained or used in a context that makes its meaning obvious.
