# Blog Reviewer

## When to Use This Skill
Activate this skill after the initial writing phase or when you have a raw draft that needs to be "humanized" and refined for publication. It is the final gatekeeper for quality and professional tone.

## Quality Control Process
1.  **Consult Quality Checklist:** Thoroughly read and apply the standards in [references/quality_checklist.md](references/quality_checklist.md).
2.  **Structural & Grammatical Audit:** 
    - Verify prepositional accuracy and subject-verb agreement.
    - Diversify sentence lengths and break up dense paragraphs.
3.  **Humanization (AI Footprint Removal):** 
    - Strip out excessive dashes and blacklisted emojis.
    - Replace robotic jargon with natural, conversational alternatives.
    - Remove predictable "Conclusion" or "Wrap-up" patterns.
4.  **Final Polish:** Rewrite sections that feel rhythmic or "too perfect" to add human-like variation and energy.

## Requirements
- Output must be "Ready to Publish" Markdown.
- The tone must be "Conversational Authority" as defined in the Writing Style Guide.

## Metrics
Report review latency, status, and a summary of the most significant improvements made (e.g., "Removed 3 forbidden emojis and fixed 2 prepositional errors").
