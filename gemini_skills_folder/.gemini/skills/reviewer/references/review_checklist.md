# Review Checklist

Use this checklist to evaluate every piece of written content. Flag any violations as specific revision requests.

---

## 1. Paragraph Structure

- [ ] Each paragraph covers **one main idea** only.
- [ ] Paragraphs are **3–5 sentences** long. Break up anything longer.
- [ ] Opening sentence of each paragraph clearly signals the topic.
- [ ] No wall-of-text blocks — if a paragraph exceeds 6 lines, it must be split.
- [ ] Transition sentences connect paragraphs naturally, not with forced connectors.

## 2. Paragraph Coherence

- [ ] Sentences within a paragraph follow a logical progression (claim → evidence → implication).
- [ ] No abrupt topic jumps mid-paragraph.
- [ ] Pronouns and references are unambiguous — the reader always knows what "this" or "it" refers to.
- [ ] Each paragraph earns its place — remove any that repeat a point already made.

## 3. AI Fingerprint Detection

Flag the content as `NEEDS_REVISION` if it exhibits **three or more** of the following AI-generated patterns:

### Punctuation & Formatting Tells

- [ ] **Dash overuse**: AI text over-relies on em dashes (—) and en dashes (–) where a colon, semicolon, comma, or period would be more natural. Check that the content uses a healthy mix of punctuation — not dashes as the default connector.
- [ ] **Colon/semicolon avoidance**: Human writers naturally use colons to introduce lists or explanations and semicolons to join related clauses. If the content has zero or near-zero colons and semicolons but many dashes, flag it.
- [ ] **Excessive bold/italic formatting**: AI tends to bold key phrases aggressively. Bold should be used sparingly for genuine emphasis, not on every other sentence.

### Word Choice & Tone Tells

- [ ] **Overused AI vocabulary**: Flag heavy use of words humans rarely use in casual or professional blog writing, such as:
  - "delve", "dive deep", "deep dive"
  - "landscape" (when not literal geography)
  - "leverage" (as a verb, overused)
  - "robust", "seamless", "cutting-edge"
  - "paradigm", "synergy", "holistic"
  - "revolutionize", "game-changer", "unprecedented"
  - "navigate" (metaphorical overuse)
  - "tapestry", "realm", "beacon"
  - "foster", "spearhead", "underscores"
  - "it's worth noting", "it's important to note"
- [ ] **Filler hedging phrases**: Remove or rewrite lines like "In today's rapidly evolving world", "In the ever-changing landscape of", "As we stand on the brink of". Real writers get to the point.
- [ ] **Superlative stacking**: Avoid clustering words like "truly remarkable", "incredibly powerful", "absolutely essential". One strong adjective is enough.

### Structural Tells

- [ ] **Overly uniform paragraph length**: If every paragraph is nearly the same length (e.g., all 4 sentences), vary them. Real writing has natural rhythm — some paragraphs are 2 sentences, some are 5.
- [ ] **Predictable heading patterns**: AI often produces headings in rigid parallel form (e.g., "The Power of X", "The Power of Y", "The Power of Z"). Headings should feel natural, not templated.
- [ ] **List-heavy writing**: If the content relies on bullet lists for the majority of its body, flag it. Lists are tools, not substitutes for prose.
- [ ] **Emoji usage**: Professional blog content should have **zero emojis** unless the brand voice explicitly calls for them. Flag any emoji use.

### Flow & Voice Tells

- [ ] **Missing contractions**: Human writers use "don't", "it's", "won't" naturally. AI often writes "do not", "it is", "will not" throughout. The content should use contractions where conversational tone is appropriate.
- [ ] **Robotic transitions**: Flag transitions like "Furthermore", "Moreover", "Additionally", "In conclusion" when overused. Real writing varies its connective tissue.
- [ ] **No personality or opinion**: If every sentence is neutral and hedged, the writing feels machine-generated. Strong writing occasionally takes a stance, uses humor, or includes a concrete personal/anecdotal reference.

---

## How to Use This Checklist

1. Read the full content once without marking anything.
2. On the second pass, check each item above.
3. For every failed check, add a specific **Revision Request** citing the paragraph or line and what needs to change.
4. If 3+ items from the "AI Fingerprint Detection" section fail, set the verdict to `NEEDS_REVISION` regardless of overall quality score.
